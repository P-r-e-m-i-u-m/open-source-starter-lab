# Contributors

Thank you for contributing to this project! You can add a short profile card to introduce yourself.

Create your card at `contributors/your-github-username.md` using this template:

## Contributor Card Template

Use this short template to introduce yourself:

- **Name:** Your name
- **Role:** Your role or current focus
- **GitHub:** [@your-username](https://github.com/your-username)
- **Pronouns:** Optional
- **Bio:** A short introduction (1–2 lines).
- **Areas of Interest:** Topics or skills you're interested in.

**Privacy reminder:** Keep your card professional.Do not share private contact details, such as personal phone numbers, home addresses, or personal email addresses.
