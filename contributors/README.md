# Contributors

Thank you for contributing to this project! You can add a short profile card to introduce yourself.

Create your card at `contributors/your-github-username.md` using this template:

## Contributor Card Template

**Name:** Your name
**Role:** Your role or current focus
**GitHub:** [@your-username](https://github.com/your-username)
**Pronouns:** Optional
**Bio:** A short introduction about yourself (1–2 lines).
**Areas:** Topics or skills you're interested in.


Note:
- Keep your card short and professional. Do not share private contact details such as personal phone numbers,home addresses, or personal email addresses.
- You can customize the fields to introduce yourself while keeping your card simple and professional.
